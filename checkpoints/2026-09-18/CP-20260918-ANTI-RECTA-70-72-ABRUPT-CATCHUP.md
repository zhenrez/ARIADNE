## New discoveries

### 70/72 network

The research has converged on a tightly coupled but non-identical family of 70/72 structures:

- Numbers 11: seventy elders, with Eldad and Medad outside the gathered group but prophesying.
- b. Sanhedrin 17a: a 72-candidate / 70-seat lottery model using six candidates from each of twelve tribes and two blank lots.
- Sanhedrin 17a preserves three rival Eldad/Medad prophecy traditions: Moses dies/Joshua succeeds; quail; Gog/Magog.
- Rav Naḥman's Gog/Magog branch explicitly uses an `al tiqre` decoder on Ezekiel `שנים`: years → two, then identifies the “two prophets” as Eldad and Medad.
- The same Sanhedrin folio places knowledge of `keshafim` beside knowledge of the seventy languages and contains a speaking-versus-understanding distinction.
- Aristeas claims 12×6 = 72 translators but the surviving roster enumerates only 71.
- Epiphanian/Syriac reception supplies a six-name Judah group that differs positionally from Aristeas rather than merely filling one blank.
- Later translation reception includes 72 isolated translators, 36 pairs/cells, and Christian rounding to seventy; these architectures are retained as distinct transforms.
- Luke 10 has a genuine 70/72 textual variant and two-by-two sending, producing a 35/36-pair consequence depending on the reading.
- The overall research priority has shifted from asking only “why 70/72?” toward identifying **which object is being counted**: addresses, entities, pairs, roles, realizations, states, or textual witnesses.

### Source-dependency and chronology

- Josephus is treated as substantially dependent on Aristeas for the translation narrative rather than independent corroboration.
- Jerome is valuable as an ancient dependency critic who rejects the separate-cell legend as absent from Aristeas/Josephus.
- Historical Theodotion is distinguished from older “Theodotionic” / kaige material; reading age ≠ named translator age.
- Aquila/Onkelos and Theodotion/Proto-Theodotion remain entity-resolution and chronology gates.
- Irenaeus/Tertullian/Epiphanius repetitions are not automatically counted as independent witness lines.
- Colarbasus remains a high-value disputed case where a textual/semantic expression may have been reified as a person-name in transmission.
